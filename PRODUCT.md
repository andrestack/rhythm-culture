# Rhythm Culture: product context

register: brand

**Who:** Rhythm Culture (Nathaniel Combs), West African drum and dance on the Sunshine Coast, QLD. School, kindy and vacation-care incursions, corporate team building, weekly classes, Camp Afro Foli, a small shop.

**Who reads the site:** teachers, early-learning directors, OSHC coordinators and HR/event organisers choosing an incursion or workshop, usually on a phone between other jobs; plus local adults looking for classes. The page has to answer "is this right for my group, and how do I get a quote" fast.

**Goal of every sales page:** a quote or booking enquiry (`/quick-quote/`, `/booking-form/`). Enquiries are the business's revenue.

**Design brief (Oct 2026):** keep the current WordPress/Divi look; the rebuild restores its section structure (photo heroes, columns, icon features, testimonial cards, the colour-stripe banner) as reusable Markdoc blocks the owner can edit with Keystatic or ChatGPT. Not a redesign.

**Brand:** red `#e02b20` (buttons, section titles), green `#7cda24` (decoration and on-photo headings only, never text on white), ink `#282828`, Assegai display headings, Open Sans text. Real photos of kids and adults drumming carry the energy; the multicoloured stripe banner is the signature.

**Editors:** non-developers via Keystatic (`/keystatic`) and ChatGPT/Codex (`.agents/skills/rhythm-culture-cms`, block reference in `blocks.md`).
