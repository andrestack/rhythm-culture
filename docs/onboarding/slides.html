Website editor · changing text

Edit the words, then Save

  1. Open Pages (or Blog posts) and click the page
  2. Click into the text and type, just like a document. Coloured boxes are layout blocks (banners, columns, testimonials): edit the words inside them, leave the boxes
  3. Click Save. It goes live about 2 minutes later

Leave URL path alone: changing it changes the page's address and breaks Google links.

Website editor · adding photos

Photos inside a page

  1. Click in the text where the photo should go
  2. Use the insert image button in the editor toolbar and pick the photo from your computer
  3. Type a short description (who, what, where). Google and screen readers use it
  4. Save

Phone photos are huge (3–8 MB) and slow the site down. For big photos, banners and product photos, give them to Claude instead: it shrinks them first.

  4. Say "publish". Nothing goes live before that

Building the shop with Claude

  1. Log in to your Stripe account (Stripe takes the payments)
  2. Pick which products to sell first, and confirm each price
  3. Claude creates a Stripe Payment Link for each one, with your OK
  4. The link goes into the product: its button changes from Enquire to Buy
  5. Together we tidy how products look in the shop

Bring: Stripe login, the price list, and which products are drums (they need postage).

Claude connectors

Let Claude see your other tools

Connectors let Claude read and help with your accounts when you ask. Only add what you'll use:

  • Gmail: "summarise this week's enquiries", "draft a reply to Maddy"
  • Google Drive: the enquiries Sheet, photos and documents
  • Google Calendar: "am I free on 19 January?"
  • Stripe: for the shop workshop (Payment Links)
How to add one
  1. In the Claude app, open Settings › Connectors
  2. Click Browse connectors and choose one (e.g. Gmail)
  3. Click Connect, sign in to that account and Allow
  4. In a chat, switch it on from the + / tools menu when you need it

We'll add them together. You can disconnect any connector in the same place.
